CULTURAL
Programs that emphasize and/educate the Chapter or 
Community on African American History and Culture. 
Examples of focus opportunites:
• MLK Day of Service
• HBCU Activation
• Celebrating Black Leaders, Authors, Inventors, etc.
EDUCATION
Empowering Our Future Leaders Through:
• Leadership Development
Leadership Develop Curriculum (Grades 6-12)
• Financial Literacy
Financial Literacy Modules (Grades 3-12)
Activities focused on Financial Acumen
• STE[A]M

HEALTH
Programs and activities that focus on and emphasize healthy 
living habits:
• Mental/Emotional
• Nutritional
• Physical
• Dental
CIVIC
Programs that focus on serving and advocating for ALL 
Children through:
Legislative - Programs that emphasize and/or support the 
importance of legislative advocacy (grade group or chapter-
wide)  
• Voter Advocacy, Gun Violence Policy & Prevention, 
Support the CROWN Act
• Education Equity, Policing Reform, Mental Health    
Advocacy, Environmental Justice, Tobacco and Menthol-
flavored products
Community Service - Programs that focus on direct services 
to the local African American Community and/or is aligned 
with a National Community Impact Partner. Focus on 
organizations that impact:
• Mental Health
• Food Insecurities
• Homelessness
• Maternal and Fetal Health
• Human Trafficking Awareness and Prevention
SOCIAL/RECREATIONAL
Social Programs that focus on healthy social interactions.  
• Each grade group should engage in at least one activity 
that promoes healthy social engagement.
• Ice Breakers, Gavel Club, Toastmaster, Oratorical 
Masters
• Interviewing Techniques, Public Speaking
• How to Safely use Social Media 
Recreational Programs that focus on indoor/outdoor 
activities for the purpose of exercise, relaxation, or fun.  
• Each grade group should engage in at least one activity 
that is outdoors.
• Explore Nature, Hiking, Ziplining, Sailing and Tennis.
