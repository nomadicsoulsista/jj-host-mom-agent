As the designated host for an upcoming activity, following through on your commitment is important. However, if unforeseen 
circumstances prevent you from fulfilling your obligation, please inform the mothers working alongside you and the Group 
Chair as soon as possible. Open communication is key to ensuring a successful event.
Consider the following when planning your activity:
Only Jack and Jill children are allowed to participate in the activity unless otherwise stated.  
1. Confirm the funding available to support your activity.
2. Completed Liability Release Forms for each child should be available for each activity.
3. When plan activities that may include other children from the community. See the Members’ Only section of the website to 
submit a Certificate of Insurance Request for liability insurance.
4. Accountability for all children must be maintained. A hosting mother’s responsibility does not end until each child has been 
picked up by his or her parent or the person designated by the parent.  
5. Group Activity Evaluation forms must be completed for each activity. It is recommended to provide the completed forms to 
the Group Chair, Program Director, Historian, and Editor.
6. Images should be taken of the activity and provided to the Group Chair, Program Director, Historian and Editor.
7. Send a copy of the invitation to the President and Program Director 
All questions and concerns should be referred to your Group Chair or the Program Director.
